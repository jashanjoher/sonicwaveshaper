ABOUT OUR BRAND

Built by producers, for producers.

Sonic Audio Labs makes tools that sit between a physical pedalboard and a modern session. We start from the way real amplifiers and stompboxes behave, then build the digital version with DSP that stays precise at high drive.

Waveshaper's core is neural amp modeling (NAM), which learns the response of real gear from captured audio. The plugin pairs that with a saturation stage designed to avoid harsh aliasing, so high gain stays musical rather than fizzy.

The look is deliberate too: a vintage stompbox faceplate, tactile knobs, and a palette that makes the tool feel like hardware on your desk.

Analog warmth

Saturation curves shaped to sound like driven tube and transistor circuits, not sterile clipping.

Neural modeling

NAM-based models capture real amplifier behaviour. Load your own .nam profile or use the built-in default.

DSP precision

Oversampled processing and careful gain staging keep the top end clean at high drive settings.
