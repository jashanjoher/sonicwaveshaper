About Maya DSP

Engineering for producers working at high gain and high tempo.

Maya DSP makes audio plugins for psytrance, hi-tech, breakcore, metal, and bass music. Each tool is built around three areas: analog-style saturation, neural modeling of real amplifiers, and DSP that keeps the top end clean when drive is pushed.

SonicWaveshaper is the first product. Its neural engine runs captured amplifier models, and you can load your own .nam profiles to change the tone.

Analog-style saturation

A waveshaping stage with adjustable drive, mix, and output, designed for controlled clipping rather than harsh digital distortion.

Neural tone

NAM-based models capture the behaviour of real tube amplifiers. Load a .nam profile or use the built-in default.

Transient control

Dynamics tools for kick and bass that keep attack intact at fast tempos. Currently in development.
