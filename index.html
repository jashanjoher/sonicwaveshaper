ABOUT MAYA DSP

Vedic psychoacoustics. Cybernetic sound design.

Maya DSP builds tools that treat sound as something to be shaped with care. The ethos draws on Vedic ideas about how sound affects perception, and on cybernetic sound design that uses neural models of real gear.

Our promise is simple: zero aliasing, brutal analog warmth, and zero digital fizz. The saturation stage is designed to keep high drive musical instead of harsh.

Zero aliasing

Saturation is designed to avoid the harsh artefacts that alias at high drive. Claims about oversampling and antiderivative methods should be checked against the shipping build.

Analog warmth

Neural tube-preamp modeling via NAM captures real amplifier behaviour, not a generic clipper.

Built for producers

Tight tools for fast tempos, heavy genres, and surgical mix control.
